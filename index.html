Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Learn more about MR Print Hub's transfer modes, security, and printer support.

How do the two transfer modes work (Local Wi-Fi vs Mobile Internet)? ▾
MR Print Hub supports two flexible transfer methods: (1) Local Wi-Fi transfers files directly over your shop's local network at maximum LAN speed without using internet data. (2) Mobile Internet allows customers who aren't connected to your shop Wi-Fi to upload documents using their own cellular data (4G/5G). Both modes deliver files directly to your MR Print Hub dashboard.
Does the customer need to install any mobile app? ▾
No. Customers simply scan your shop's QR code using their smartphone's native camera. The QR code opens a lightweight browser-based upload page. They select their documents and hit Send — no App Store downloads or accounts needed.
What file formats are supported? ▾
MR Print Hub supports standard document and image formats including PDF, Microsoft Word (.docx), and high-resolution images (JPG, PNG). Files are transferred in their original uncompressed resolution.
Can multiple customers send files at the same time? ▾
Yes! Multiple walk-in customers can scan the QR code and submit documents simultaneously. Each customer is assigned a unique session identifier (#CUST token) that appears in your Live Upload Stream.
Can I use my existing printers? ▾
Yes. MR Print Hub integrates directly with Windows printing services and works seamlessly with all existing USB, Network, and Wireless printers configured on your computer.
Is MR Print Hub free to download? ▾
Yes, MR Print Hub v1.0.0 is free to download and use on your Windows computer with no hidden subscription fees.
